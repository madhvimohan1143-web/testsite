Introduction – What is Journalism & Mass Communication?

Journalism and Mass Communication is the study of collecting, analyzing, and delivering news and information to the public through newspapers, TV, radio, and digital media platforms.

It is one of the most influential careers because media shapes public opinion, government awareness, and social awareness.

Types of Journalism in Modern Industry

  • Print Journalism: Newspapers, magazines
  • Broadcast Journalism: TV news channels
  • Digital Journalism: Online news portals
  • Investigative Journalism: Deep research reporting
  • Sports Journalism: Sports coverage & analysis

Real Skills Required in Journalism Industry

  • Strong communication skills
  • Research & fact-checking ability
  • Confidence in speaking
  • Writing speed & clarity
  • Interview handling skills

Step-by-Step Career Roadmap

  • Step 1: Complete 12th (any stream)
  • Step 2: Choose BA in Journalism / Mass Communication
  • Step 3: Start internship in media house
  • Step 4: Learn editing tools & digital media
  • Step 5: Build portfolio (news articles/videos)
  • Step 6: Apply for reporter/anchor/content roles

Real Industry Workflow (How News is Made)

Step 1: Reporter collects news
Step 2: Fact checking is done
Step 3: Editor verifies content
Step 4: News is formatted
Step 5: Published on TV/website/newspaper

Salary Scope

Fresh journalists earn ₹20,000–₹50,000/month. Experienced journalists and anchors can earn ₹1 lakh+ depending on media house and role.

Common Mistakes Students Make

  • Thinking journalism is only TV reporting
  • Ignoring writing practice
  • No internship experience
  • Weak communication skills

Future of Journalism

Digital journalism is growing faster than print media. Social media news, YouTube journalism, and independent reporting are future trends.
