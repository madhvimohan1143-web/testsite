NDA Career Guide After 12th Arts (2026)

Eligibility, Syllabus, Preparation Strategy & Defense Career Growth

📌 Introduction

12th Arts ke baad NDA ek powerful aur respected career option hai jisme aap Army officer ban sakte ho. Yeh India ka sabse prestigious defense entry exam hai.

Agar aap discipline, respect, uniform life aur government job chahte ho, toh NDA best choice hai.

---

🎯 NDA Eligibility (Arts Students)

  • 12th pass (Arts allowed for Army Wing)
  • Age: 16.5 – 19.5 years
  • Only unmarried candidates
  • Physical fitness required

Note: Navy & Air Force ke liye Maths + Physics required hota hai.

---

📝 NDA Exam Pattern

  • Maths – 300 marks
  • General Ability Test (GAT) – 600 marks
  • Total – 900 marks
  • SSB Interview – 900 marks
---

🏆 Career Opportunities After NDA

1. Indian Army Officer

Lieutenant se start karke General tak promotion milta hai.

2. Indian Navy (Limited for Arts)

Only if eligibility matches (rare case).

3. Indian Air Force

Not for pure Arts students.

📈 NDA Preparation Strategy

🔹 Daily Study Plan

  • 2 hrs – Maths basics
  • 2 hrs – GK + Current Affairs
  • 1 hr – English
  • 1 hr – Physical fitness

🔹 Smart Strategy

  • NCERT books complete karo
  • Previous papers solve karo
  • Mock tests weekly do
  • SSB interview preparation start karo

🔹 Physical Preparation

  • Running (1–2 km daily)
  • Push-ups & sit-ups
  • Medical fitness maintain karo
