What are Central Universities?

Central universities are established by an Act of Parliament and are funded by the Government of India. Admission is mainly through CUET (Common University Entrance Test).

Admission Process (Step-by-Step)

  1. Register for CUET (UG)
  2. Select universities & courses
  3. Appear for exam
  4. Check results & cutoff
  5. Participate in counselling
  6. Document verification & admission
