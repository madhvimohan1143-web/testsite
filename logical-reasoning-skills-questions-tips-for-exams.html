Introduction – Why Logical Reasoning is Important

Logical reasoning is one of the most scoring sections in competitive exams like SSC, Banking, UPSC, Railway, and entrance exams. It tests how well a student can think logically without memorizing answers.

Unlike theory subjects, reasoning requires practice, pattern recognition, and fast thinking ability.

What is Logical Reasoning?

Logical reasoning is the ability to solve problems using structured thinking, patterns, relationships, and logic instead of random guessing.

Types of Logical Reasoning Questions

  • Number Series: Find missing number pattern
  • Alphabet Series: Letter-based patterns
  • Coding-Decoding: Symbol or word conversion
  • Blood Relations: Family relationship logic
  • Syllogism: Statement-based conclusions
  • Direction Sense: Left-right movement problems

How to Approach Any Reasoning Question

  • Read question carefully (don’t rush)
  • Identify pattern or hidden rule
  • Eliminate wrong options
  • Use step-by-step logic
  • Double check final answer

Exam-Level Strategy

In exams, time management is more important than knowledge. Solve easy questions first, then move to difficult ones.

Most students fail in reasoning not because it is hard, but because they don’t recognize patterns quickly.

Real Exam Example

If A is brother of B, B is mother of C, then A becomes uncle of C. This type of chain logic is frequently asked in exams.

Common Mistakes Students Make

  • Not reading question properly
  • Assuming answers without logic
  • Spending too much time on one question
  • Not practicing regularly

Daily Practice Plan

  • 30 minutes reasoning practice daily
  • Mock tests every 3 days
  • Analyze wrong answers
  • Improve speed gradually
